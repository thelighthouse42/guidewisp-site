Privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

The short version

  • You don't need an account. Each install gets an anonymous random install id.
  • To answer a question, it goes through our server to Anthropic (the AI model that writes the answer) and Tavily (web search).
  • Sharing anonymous usage is off until you choose to turn it on.
  • Answer reports are only sent when you tap Report.
  • We never collect your game account details, keys or screenshots.
  • Your settings are stored on your own PC.

Who we are

GuideWisp is made by an independent solo developer. In this policy, “GuideWisp”, “we” and “us” mean [LEGAL NAME / ADDRESS]. You can reach us any time at hello@guidewisp.com.

This policy covers the GuideWisp app for Windows and this website.

When you ask a question

To find your answer, the app sends the following to our server:

  • your question, and the recent messages from the same conversation so follow-up questions make sense;
  • your edition and the player profile details you've filled in, such as faction, class, level and professions (and your character name or realm, if you entered them);
  • your anonymous install id, so we can count your questions against your plan's allowance.

Our server runs on Supabase, hosted in the United States. It passes the question to the Anthropic API, which writes the answer, and to Tavily, which searches game guides and other public web pages. Our server keeps a count of how many questions each install id has asked and when, so it can apply plan limits. It doesn't save the text of your questions unless you turn on usage sharing or send a report (both described below).

Anthropic and Tavily process what they receive under their own terms and privacy policies.

The optional screen read

When the app starts, you can let it read your character details (like level, class and faction) from your screen to fill in your player profile. This happens on your PC. The screenshot never leaves your PC, and only the profile values you keep are used.

Optional anonymous usage sharing

Usage sharing is off until you choose to turn it on. If you do, the app sends us:

  • your install id and app version;
  • the text of your questions;
  • which sources were used for each answer;
  • errors and timings.

We use this to see what players ask, find answers that go wrong, and make GuideWisp better. You can turn it off at any time in the app's settings (“Share anonymous usage”).

Answer reports

If you tap Report a problem on an answer, the app sends us the question, the answer, its sources, your edition, faction and level, your app version, the reason you picked and any note you wrote. Nothing is sent unless you tap Report. Reports are read by hand so we can fix bad answers.

What we never collect

  • Game account details.
  • Keys.
  • Screenshots.

GuideWisp also never reads the game's memory and doesn't interact with the game client.

What stays on your PC

Your settings, including your player profile, are stored locally on your PC.

Payments

Paid plans aren't open yet. When they are, payments will be handled by Stripe. Stripe collects the payment details it needs under its own privacy policy, and we never see or store your full card number.
